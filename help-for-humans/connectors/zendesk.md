# Zendesk

**Not available right now.** Rebel doesn't currently offer a Zendesk connector — you
won't find it in **Settings → Connectors**. We pulled it while we rework how it
connects, and we'd rather ship nothing than hand you a "Set up" button that dead-ends.

When it's back, Zendesk will let you search, create, update, and comment on support
tickets, and browse views, groups, and organizations — all from Rebel.

Want it sooner? A nudge via **Help → Feedback & bugs** genuinely helps us prioritise.

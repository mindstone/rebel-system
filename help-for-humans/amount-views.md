# Amount flow views

**Coming soon** — Amount flow views are not in a released version of Rebel yet.

Ask Rebel to show how amounts move between categories or stages — for example, spending by category, a revenue bridge, a funnel, or time use. An amount funnel shows quantities moving through stages or categories; it is not a process diagram showing steps or responsibilities.

## Choose a view

- **Sankey flow** — Shows how amounts branch, join, and move between categories or stages. Wider bands represent larger amounts.
- **Signed waterfall (ups and downs)** — Shows how increases and decreases take a starting amount to an ending amount. It is useful for seeing what contributed to the change.

The best view depends on the question. A Sankey flow is useful when you want to follow paths; a signed waterfall is useful when you want to understand a total changing step by step.

## Inspect the amounts

Hover over, select, or tap an amount to see the original entries included in that view, such as a purchase or time entry. Their details may be empty or incomplete. Rebel does not invent missing details, and inspecting an amount never changes the source data or automatically fetches more information.

To change an original entry, ask Rebel in the conversation. Rebel uses access you have already given and asks for confirmation only when the existing permissions require it. To redraw or update the view, ask Rebel in the conversation for an updated view. Inspecting a view by itself does not refresh it.

The upcoming amount flow views are designed to be graphical and interactive on desktop, web, and phone.

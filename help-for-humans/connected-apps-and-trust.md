# Connected apps and trust

When a connected app (such as a business tool you have linked to Rebel) shows something interactive directly inside your conversation, Rebel makes sure you always know where it came from.

## Safe view: knowing what's yours and what's not

When an app renders an interactive view inside your conversation, the view is labelled **"From X · Safe view"** — where X is the name of the connected app. This is Rebel-owned chrome: the label appears on the view itself so you can see at a glance that it came from that specific app, not from Rebel.

An app cannot read this label or change it. The label is a Rebel guarantee, not something an app can influence or spoof.

## Buttons and approvals

Clicking a card's button approves that one action. It does not give the app permission to keep acting. Your Blocks, tools you've turned off and your organisation's restrictions still apply.

Requests a card makes in the background use your ordinary Safety Rules and saved permissions, and appear in the notification drawer when a decision is needed. The card then says: **"Nothing has happened yet. This needs your OK: open the bell at the top of Rebel and approve it there."** You can also click **View** in the approval bar at the bottom of the conversation to open the notification drawer and review the request; the outcome appears in the conversation.

To stop a tool action, use **Block** in the notification drawer or turn the tool off in **[Settings → Connectors](rebel://settings/tools)**. The separate **Connected app permissions** panel has retired. Your Safety Rules and saved permissions live in **[Settings → Privacy & Safety](rebel://settings/safety)**.

A card cannot send a message to Rebel or share extra context in the background; those actions need your input in the card. Only connect apps you trust: Rebel checks that input reached the card, but cannot check that a card honestly showed what it was about to send.

## Turning individual tools on or off

To keep an app connected but switch off one of its tools, in **[Settings → Connectors](rebel://settings/tools)**, open a connected app and toggle individual tools. For example, keep "read email" and "draft email" on while turning "send email" off. A tool you switch off simply can't be used.

You may also see tools your **organisation** has switched off for everyone — these show a shield icon and a "Disabled" badge, and can't be turned back on from your machine. That's an admin control rather than a personal one; see [Teams and admin controls](library://rebel-system/help-for-humans/teams-and-admin-controls.md). For how tool approvals work more generally, see [Security and tool safety](library://rebel-system/help-for-humans/security-and-tool-safety.md).

## Keep it manageable

Only connect apps you actively use. If you connected something for a one-off task and haven't used it since, consider removing its access — it's one less thing to keep track of.

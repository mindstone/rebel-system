# Apps

An app is a page, dashboard or small tool you keep in Rebel. Ask for a weekly report, a meeting dashboard or a focus timer: describe what you want, and Rebel builds it. Pages open in the same app view. There is one place to use them, without needing to know how they were made.

## Make or change an app

Try “Make me a dashboard of this week's meetings” or “Build a checklist that remembers what I've ticked.” Rebel checks for an existing app first, so asking to change your dashboard updates that dashboard.

For a view in a conversation, choose **Save as app** to keep it. Find your apps in **Library → Apps** (Library is in the sidebar). Pin one to **Your apps**, the list in the sidebar, to keep it close, or add it to Home, the first screen Rebel opens on. **Ask Rebel to change it** starts a conversation about that app; you send the request when you're ready.

## Keep it current

An app shows saved data until it is refreshed. The date tells you when the data was fetched or the app was saved. Opening an app does not by itself fetch new data unless you have asked for updates on open.

Ask Rebel to set up updates when you want them: on a schedule or when you open the app. Updates run through [Automations](library://rebel-system/help-for-humans/automations.md), Rebel's scheduled tasks, where you can change or stop them. Rebel never schedules updates just because you saved an app. When an app has a refresh automation, **Refresh** runs it now.

If an update needs your OK, review the request in Rebel. If an update fails, the last saved data stays visible with a notice.

## Share an app

Move or copy an app into a shared [Space](library://rebel-system/help-for-humans/spaces.md), a folder you share with your team, through the Library's sharing action. Each person uses their own sign-ins for Connectors, the accounts you link to Rebel, such as your calendar or email; sharing an app never shares your accounts. An app may keep personal data for each viewer or shared data for everyone in that Space.

The first time an app reads your accounts or acts for you, Rebel may ask you to allow it, saying what it wants to do; you can say no. Your Safety Rules, the limits you set on what Rebel may do without asking, still apply to its actions. See [Connectors and trust](library://rebel-system/help-for-humans/connectors-and-trust.md).

## Your earlier apps

If you made plugins (small tools built in earlier versions of Rebel), they are apps now, with their data. If you never made one, nothing here changes for you. Open them from Library or Your apps as before. If something works differently, for example a button that needs your OK again, the app says what changed. If it cannot open, its earlier code is still in your Library and **Ask Rebel to rebuild it** lets you request a replacement. Rebel does not rebuild it unless you ask.

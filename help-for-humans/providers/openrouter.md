# OpenRouter

**What it is:** One account, many models — reach Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok and more through a single OpenRouter connection. A first-class bring-your-own choice.

**While a Mindstone plan is in use:** models from this connection use your plan's allowance first. Here, **in use** means the plan's row in **Settings → Agent & Voice** says **In use**. Local services stay on your computer, and company-managed models stay company-paid. Pay-per-use prices apply when this connection pays.

**If your company provides OpenRouter:** there's nothing to connect. Your admin gives you your own OpenRouter key from the Transformation Engine, and Rebel collects it on its own. If it shows "Checking organisation connection..." for more than a few minutes, update Rebel and open Settings. Details: [Company-provided keys](rebel://library/rebel-system%2Fhelp-for-humans%2Fcompany-provided-keys.md).

**How to connect your own account:** In [Settings → Agent & Voice](rebel://settings/agents), choose **Add an account or key** → **Sign in with OpenRouter** and sign in — no API key to paste.

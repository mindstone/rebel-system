# ChatGPT Pro

**What it is:** Connect your existing ChatGPT account by signing in — no API key needed. Uses your ChatGPT Pro subscription to power Rebel. One of the three first-class bring-your-own choices.

**How to connect:** In [Settings → Agent & Voice](rebel://settings/agents), choose **Add an account or key** → **Sign in with ChatGPT** and sign in with your ChatGPT account.

**Useful links:**
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/)
- [ChatGPT pricing](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing/)


**Which models:** whatever your ChatGPT plan offers, including GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6.1 Terra, and the same list in every job picker (Planning, Everyday work, Behind the scenes, and For long conversations), each model shown once. The most capable models use more of your allowance.
